# Podcast player

Build the now-playing screen and the queue for "Hollowpine", a podcast app for people who listen on
their commute.

Show:
- the current episode with cover placeholder, show name, episode title and a seek bar with elapsed and
  remaining time (episode length 47 minutes);
- play or pause, skip back 15 seconds, skip forward 30 seconds, and playback speed (0.8x to 2x);
- a sleep timer with choices of 15, 30 or 60 minutes and "End of episode";
- an "Up next" queue of six episodes, each with title, show, length and a download state
  (downloaded, downloading or not downloaded), where episodes can be removed or moved up;
- a compact mini-player state that appears when the person leaves the now-playing screen.

It should be comfortable to use with one hand.
