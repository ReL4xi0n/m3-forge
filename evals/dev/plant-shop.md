# Plant shop catalog

Build the catalog page of "Greenleafe", an online shop for houseplants.

Include:
- a search field and a cart icon with a count badge that updates when something is added;
- filters for light (low, medium, bright), pet safety (pet-safe only), size (small, medium, large)
  and a price range up to 80 EUR, with a way to clear them all and a count of matching plants;
- a sort control (popular, price low to high, price high to low, newest);
- a grid of at least twelve plants, each with image placeholder, common name, price, light need and
  an "Add to basket" action; a few are "Out of stock" or "Only 2 left";
- an empty result state when no plant matches.

It should be pleasant to browse on a phone and on a wide screen.
