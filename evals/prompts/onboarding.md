# Onboarding form

Build the sign-up and onboarding flow for "Fieldnote", a note-taking app for field researchers.

Steps:
1. Create account: full name, email, password (with strength feedback) and acceptance of the terms.
2. About you: role (student, researcher, teacher or other), organization (optional) and main field
   of work, chosen from a list of about fifteen fields.
3. Preferences: units (metric or imperial), whether to back up notes automatically, and whether to
   receive a monthly tips email.
4. Done: a short welcome message with a button that opens the app.

Show progress through the steps, let people go back, validate fields with clear error messages,
and keep the entered data when moving between steps.
