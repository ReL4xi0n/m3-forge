# Marketing landing page

Build the landing page for "Tidewell", a small startup that sells a smart water bottle. The bottle
tracks how much you drink and reminds you through a phone app.

The page needs:
- a top section with the product name, a one-line pitch, a short supporting sentence, a primary
  "Pre-order" action and a secondary "See how it works" action;
- three feature highlights (hydration tracking, gentle reminders, 24-hour cold);
- a "how it works" part in three steps;
- two customer quotes;
- a pricing section with two plans (Bottle only, Bottle + app premium for a year);
- a short FAQ with four questions;
- a footer with links (About, Support, Privacy, Terms) and a newsletter sign-up field.

Make it look polished and modern, and make sure it works on both phones and large screens.
