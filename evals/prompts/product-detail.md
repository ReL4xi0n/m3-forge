# Product detail page

Build the product page for a pair of running shoes, "Strider 4", sold by an online sports shop.

Include:
- an image gallery with four images (use placeholders);
- name, price ($129) and a rating of 4.6 from 2,310 reviews;
- color choice (three colors) and size choice (EU 38 to 46; sizes 39 and 45 are sold out);
- quantity, an "Add to cart" action and a "Save to wishlist" action;
- delivery information (free delivery over $50, arrives in 2–4 days) and returns information;
- a description, a short list of specs (weight, drop, cushioning) and a section with three reviews;
- a row of four related products.

It should make buying easy and feel trustworthy.
