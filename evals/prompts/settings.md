# Settings screen

Build the settings screen for "Pocketbook", a personal budgeting app.

Group the settings into sections:
- Account: profile (name and email shown), change password, linked bank accounts (shows "3 linked").
- Notifications: a master on/off for notifications, then on/off for weekly summary, bill reminders
  and unusual spending alerts.
- Preferences: currency (currently USD), first day of the week (Monday or Sunday), theme (system,
  light or dark).
- Privacy: app lock with biometrics (on/off), hide balances on the home screen (on/off).
- About: version number, terms of service, privacy policy.
- At the bottom: a "Sign out" action and a destructive "Delete account" action that asks for
  confirmation first.

It should feel native and easy to scan.
